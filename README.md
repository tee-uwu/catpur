Cozy Paws is a relaxing, browser-based virtual pet interaction game. Take a moment out of your day to pet a virtual cat, level up your friendship bond, and enjoy a cozy, interactive environment!  
✨ FeaturesInteractive Petting: Click or tap on your cat to give them "PATS" and watch them bounce or jump happily. 
### Progression System: Track your total pats, watch your "LEVEL" increase, and fill up your "BOND LEVEL" hearts to 100%. 
### Choose Your Friend: Swap between four adorable feline friends: Tabby, Smoke, Night, and Calico.  
### Cozy Atmosphere: Enjoy a peaceful stage with animated floating clouds, reactive speech bubbles, and pop-up emojis (🌸, ✨, 💖, 🌼).
### Relaxing Sound Effects: Gentle synthesized "boop" sounds when petting, complete with a mute toggle (🔊/🔇).  
### Auto-Save: Your petting progress is automatically saved in your browser's local storage so you never lose your bond.  
### Fully Responsive: Perfectly playable on both desktop and mobile devices.  
🛠️ Tech StackThis project is built purely with vanilla web technologies. No build steps or frameworks are required!HTML5  CSS3 (Custom properties, keyframe animations, and grid/flexbox layouts)  Vanilla JavaScript (AudioContext, DOM manipulation, and LocalStorage)  Google Fonts (VT323 and Nunito)
